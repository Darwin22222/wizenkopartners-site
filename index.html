What we do

Three levers. One goal: growth that holds.

We take operational leadership where growth gets stuck: at the top, in sales, or in operations. Engagements typically run 6 to 24 months, with one goal: a system you can take back in-house.

01

General management

Interim leadership and governance.

  • 01Step in as interim general manager or deputy CEO
  • 02Prepare and de-risk leadership transitions
  • 03Run governance with boards and shareholders
  • 04Own the P&L, budget and reporting to owners and investors
02

Sales

A sales team that closes on its own.

  • 01Reposition what you sell so it reaches bigger budgets and bigger deals
  • 02Turn a gut-feel pipeline into a weighted, forecastable one
  • 03Build outbound prospecting from zero: targeting, messaging, sequences and phone
  • 04Recruit, train and structure sales teams, with hiring sized on real capacity
  • 05Take sales off the founder's plate with a CRM and playbook built around your cycle
03

Operations

Systems your teams run every day.

  • 01Give leadership clear visibility on revenue, margins, pipeline and resources
  • 02Clarify roles, responsibilities and governance on key accounts
  • 03Use AI to take repetitive work off your teams' hands
  • 04Reduce dependency on a handful of critical people
